Method. Every run sent random prompts with a fixed output length and greedy sampling (temperature 0) through vllm bench serve, or on Macs the repo's tools/loadgen.py, which sends the same requests and reports the same measurements. QuietBox 2 (2× p300c, 4 Tenstorrent Blackhole chips) served tt serve bundles, and a single p150a card served the same bundles' images on Kubernetes; DGX Sparks served vLLM in NVIDIA's container, and two Sparks ran one copy of a model over a direct QSFP link; Macs served 4-bit MLX builds with mlx_lm.server. Wall power came from a smart plug on each machine, sampled once a second and averaged over each run; tokens per joule is output tokens per second divided by that average. Running cost assumes a 30-day month at full load, the measured wall power and an editable electricity price (default $0.39/kWh); hardware isn't included. Systems: __SYSTEMS__.

Quality scores are the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3.2 as published at artificialanalysis.ai on 2026-10-13, not measured on these machines:
